When a bot writes something worth keeping, such as a hiring plan, a board deck, a cash dashboard or a CSV, it is saved as an artifact. Chief might answer "I drafted the deck; it is in Artifacts," and link to it.

![The Praci Artifacts page with a grid of documents](/docs/artifacts.webp)

*Demo data.*

## What you can do

- Browse artifacts as a grid or a list. Each card shows the file type (Markdown, CSV, HTML), a short description, which bot made it and when.
- Search by name, filter by date or by bot, and open or delete an artifact. Deleting cannot be undone.
- When a bot revises an artifact, the card shows its version, such as **v2**.

## Share a link

You can share an HTML page, a dashboard, an image or a PDF with someone who does not use Praci. Open the artifact and choose **Share**. Praci makes a link and shows it once, so copy it from the **Share link** dialog with **Copy**.

![The Share link dialog](/docs/share-dialog.webp)

*Demo data.*

- Anyone with the link can view the artifact. They cannot edit it, and they do not need to sign in.
- The link shows the version you shared. A newer version does not reach the link on its own. Open the newer version and choose **Update link** to make a new link for it. The old link stops working.
- A link works for 30 days, or until you stop it. Each artifact has one link at a time.
- **Stop sharing** in the dialog ends the link after you confirm. If you reopen the dialog later, the link is not shown again. Only **Stop sharing** is available.
- Deleting an artifact also stops its link. The delete dialog says so.

![The page a link opens, showing the artifact with the label Shared from Praci](/docs/shared-page.webp)

*Demo data.*

Other file types, such as Markdown and CSV, have no **Share** button. Bots cannot share links. Only you can.

## Versions

An artifact with more than one version has a version menu above its preview. When you pick an older version, **Restore this version** saves its content as a new version. The older versions stay.

Artifacts belong to your space. For a copy of everything you own, see [Backups and export](/docs/backups-and-export).

Artifacts differ from the [Data room](/docs/data-room). The Data room holds documents you bring in. Artifacts hold what bots produce.
